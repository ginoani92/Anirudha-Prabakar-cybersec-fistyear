What is Web3?

Web3 describes a vision of an internet where blockchain networks can enable decentralized applications, digital assets and new forms of online ownership.

🔗

Blockchain

A distributed ledger that records transactions across a network of computers.

🌍

Decentralization

Systems can distribute data, control and verification across multiple participants instead of relying on one central authority.
